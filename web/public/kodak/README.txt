Kodak Lossless True Color Image Suite (kodim01-kodim24)

24 photographs (768 x 512, six of them portrait) widely used as standard test images. They are used here as
test images in the LUT tab's preview (SPEC §14.4).

Source: https://r0k.us/graphics/kodak/ (PNG originals, downloaded 2026-11-25). The page says: "It is my
understanding they have been released by the Eastman Kodak Company for unrestricted usage."

Converted to JPEG (quality 85) to keep the app small; not otherwise changed.
