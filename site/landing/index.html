Clip Sequencer for Resolume

Play multi-layer clip sequences in Resolume Arena, locked to your show's MIDI clock. Build them on a 1/16-bar grid, start them from the app or by clicking a clip in Resolume.

What it does

  • Lanes per layer

    Clips and rests on several Resolume layers at once, on a 1/8 or 1/16-bar grid. Repeat once, ×N or endlessly. Slot lanes play whatever sits in a column, so you can swap clips live.

  • On the clock

    Follows your MIDI clock and triggers every clip exactly on its tick over OSC; Resolume confirms each one.

  • Start from Resolume

    Click a § placeholder clip on a SEQ layer, from the mouse, a controller or the dashboard, and its sequence starts.

  • Live-friendly

    Click a clip by hand and that layer is yours until you hand it back. Clock lost? Sequences wait and continue.

  • Survives your edits

    Clips are found again by ID, name or file after you move or rename them; sequences are kept per deck.

  • Timeline in Resolume

    The optional FFGL plugin "1000A Sequence" shows a sequence's lanes and playhead right on its clip.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, on the same computer as Resolume.
  • Resolume Arena 7.26 or newer, with the webserver and OSC input switched on.
  • A MIDI clock on a MIDI input (for example a virtual MIDI port).
  • The Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (part of Windows 11).
