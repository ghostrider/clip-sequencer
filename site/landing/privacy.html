Privacy

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other websites: fonts and images are served from here.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to GitHub (downloads, source, issues) lead to GitHub's own pages, where its privacy statement applies.

The app

The Clip Sequencer app sends no data anywhere. It talks only to Resolume and its own window or browser page on your computer, and keeps its files in its data folder.
